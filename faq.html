Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about orders, products, and support.

Orders & Shipping

How long does shipping take?

Orders typically ship within 1–3 business days. Delivery times vary by destination, usually 5–12 business days for domestic orders and longer for international shipments.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes — free shipping is available on orders over $130 within the continental US. International orders may include additional duties and taxes.

Can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number by email so you can follow its progress in real time.

Product & Warranty

What is the warranty period?

All GravaStar products come with a 12-month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects from the original date of purchase.

Are the switches hot-swappable?

Most of our keyboards feature hot-swappable switches, letting you customize your typing feel without soldering. Check the individual product page for details.

Do you offer software for customizing RGB and macros?

Yes, compatible products can be customized using our companion software, available under Software Download in the footer.

Returns

What is your return policy?

You can return unused products within 30 days of delivery. Contact our support team to initiate the return process.

Who pays for return shipping?

If the return is due to a product defect, we cover shipping. For personal-reason returns, customers are responsible for round-trip shipping costs.
